Shortcuts: P=Play, F=First note, T=Start, R=Reveal next, S=Show, N=Random movement.
Pick a quartet, movement and instrument. The instrument's line - the top note of a double stop, the bottom one for the cello - is split into phrases where the corpus marks a phrase ending and at the instrument's rests, with long stretches split after a long note. Play the phrase as often as you like. Start sets out the phrase's bars with only its first note showing; Reveal next adds one more note at a time. Show gives all four parts for the phrase's bars, with the phrase you were writing in blue.
