Every voicing is a stack of fourths (or fifths). The name says which rung is the odd one out — the augmented fourth in a quartal stack, the diminished fifth in a quintal one. Perfect has none, dual has both the lowest and the highest.
4-note answers: perfect lower middle upper dual. 5-note answers: perfect lower lower middle upper middle upper dual.
