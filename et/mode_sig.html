Each key signature can spell seven different modes — one for each diatonic tonic. Brightest to darkest: Lydian (+1), Ionian (0), Mixolydian (−1), Dorian (−2), Aeolian (−3), Phrygian (−4), Locrian (−5). The number is how many sharps the mode adds to (or removes from) the major key signature with the same tonic. Press Enter or Space after answering to continue.
