Shortcuts: P=Play, F=First note, T=Start, R=Reveal next, S=Show, N=Random chorale.
Type a number from 1 to 371 and press Enter, pick a key to list its chorales, or take a random one (from the chosen key, if there is one). Each chorale is split into phrases at its fermatas. Play the phrase as often as you like. Start sets out the phrase's bars on a staff with only its first note filled in; Reveal next adds one more note at a time, to check your dictation as you go. Show gives the whole chorale, with the phrase you were writing in blue. Repeats are played once.
